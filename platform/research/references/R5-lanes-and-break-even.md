## Ten jackets of 5 kg each

Ten parcels of exactly 5.0 kg packed have a published postage comparison of **PHP 79,940**. If the jackets alone each weigh 5 kg, packing sends each into at least the 5.5 kg bracket, for **at least PHP 86,960** when sent individually. A hypothetical two-parcel 25 kg + 25 kg configuration would be PHP 72,148 at the same tariff before packing. It is a sensitivity calculation only: the explicit weight/size acceptance and packing plan have to be verified. A single 50 kg parcel is never presumed acceptable. Never compare these PHP figures with CAD resale tags without a dated FX quote.

**Current action: keep the lot at origin and get a local appraisal.** The shop has supplied neither an after-fee resale floor nor an acceptance probability or a preference for financing the freight. That makes a profitable import unproved. A buyer contract, a carrier quote and actual handling/offer costs could reverse this recommendation. Local referral or replacement is a real revenue line only with a partner, inventory and terms in place.

For one item let R be achievable after-fee resale revenue in a single currency, q the chance of acceptance and sale in the stated period, P the cash acquisition cost, F the *incremental* allocated transport, D non-recoverable duty/tax and clearance, H staff and handling costs, and S the net salvage contribution on rejection. Then expected contribution is qR + (1−q)S − P − F − D − H. For q>0 break-even R = (P+F+D+H−(1−q)S)/q. Compare to the contribution of an actually available local route. With q=1 and all other costs set to zero, freight alone sets the numeric lower bound in the table; it is not a used price. A 13% duty applied to declared customs value is not automatically 13% of expected resale. Where q, amounts, FX or local comparison are missing, the product shows the missing inputs and a break-even floor, leaving total margin open. Apply non-recoverable tax in contribution and distinguish recoverable tax cash flow.

## Border rules that affect the calculation

Canadian chapter 63 note 3 specifies certain articles with appreciable wear presented in bulk, bales, sacks or similar packing. The heading's list and the material matter; individually packed leather jackets do not become 6309 merely because someone wore them. For qualifying other worn articles, 6309.00.90 displays 18% MFN; the wiping-rag line is a different end use. Under 4203.10, leather apparel has a 13% MFN reference. Preferential origin can change the rate. Treat classification and origin proofs as open inputs rather than guessing a tariff from the rider's country. Confidence high in the published lines, case-specific eligibility unverified. [CBSA chapter 63](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/trade-commerce/tariff-tarif/2026/html/02/ch63-eng.html) · [CBSA chapter 42](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/trade-commerce/tariff-tarif/2026/html/00/ch42-eng.html).

Canada's ordinary mail relief is generally CAD 20. The CUSMA thresholds of CAD 40 for taxes and CAD 150 for duties are for eligible courier imports from the US or Mexico, with exceptions; they are not the postal allowance and do not cover Philippine-origin mail by default. Canada's GST and possible handling charges depend on the entry. In the reverse direction US CBP reports the suspension of the ordinary US de minimis exemption across countries subject to stated exceptions. No automatic USD 800 allowance in this product.
