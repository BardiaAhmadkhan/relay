**Why the Belstaff hypothesis partly holds.** On 18 May 2011 the Veneto regional authority recorded an agreement transferring the Belstaff business branch from Clothing Company SpA to Belstaff Srl, effective 1 June 2011. The Canadian trademark register separately lists both the Mogliano Veneto, Via Giotto n. 5 address and the Via Enrico Stendhal, 35 address in its ownership record. This supports the historical ordering of the **companies**. It does not establish a cut-over date for sewn labels, older stock, later licensed production or the manufacture date of items 012/014/015. The engine can present the clue and its provenance and request a genuine production code. **Confidence high for the corporate fact, insufficient for a particular garment's age.** [Veneto regional record](https://www.regione.veneto.it/article-detail?articleId=359856) · [Canadian Intellectual Property Office trademark record](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search/pdf/0350689?lang=eng) · [Belstaff history](https://belstaff.com/en-eu/pages/about-us).
