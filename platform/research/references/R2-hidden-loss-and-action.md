# What can change a decision about apparently intact gear

Checked 21 November 2026. A point estimate of remaining crash protection would require traceable garment-specific evidence that these photos and studies do not supply. V4 represents the set of compatible conditions for each layer. If the same action is justified for every compatible state, use it. If two possible states require different action, show the source of that uncertainty and the observation that can resolve it. Lab values below shape the questions, never a numerical lifetime curve.

| Factor | What is supported | Engine use and remaining uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aramid and sunlight | DuPont reports loss of strength after extended UV exposure. Yarn size, fabric thickness and UV shielding affect the result. | **High** for exposed fibre and the importance of shielding. Ask about direct exposure of the actual aramid or look for exposed backing. Shell fading or a rider's country cannot quantify UV dose to a hidden liner. |
| Water, heat, pH, washing and flex | DuPont's examples separate neutral water and accelerated high-temperature/chemical exposure. Covering, weave, flex, coatings, detergents and the actual washing procedure differ across garments. | **High** that exposure conditions matter. No defensible wash-count or riding-hours cutoff for these 19. Keep the care instructions and actual rider history in separate fields. Suspected chemical, heat or care damage prompts inspection. |
| UHMWPE and Dyneema | The manufacturer's high-modulus polyethylene factsheet reports its fibre melting range as 144–152 °C. A blended yarn or finished garment is a different construction. | **High** on material range; no photo-based conversion to remaining performance. Relevant observed melting or an explicitly disclosed damaging incident is distinct from age, geography or a normal care cycle. |
| Plain, blended and lined denim | MotoCAP's construction guide reports an example of one/two/three layers of the same 400 g/m² cotton denim lasting 0.6/2/4 s on its laboratory abrasion method. Layer count, fibre, location and test method matter. | **High** for that test sample. The values cannot be applied to an unknown pair, a visible patch of cotton, or a road speed. Crop the lining, distinguish continuous material, inserts and panelled constructions. |
| Leather, seams and ageing | Full-grain, top-grain and split leathers of comparable thickness can perform differently; tanning, panel orientation and stitching change behaviour. Seam design and thread breakage influence burst strength. | **High** for laboratory/engineering mechanisms. A photo cannot measure hide thickness, hidden stitching or tensile strength. Creasing, shine or age alone do not prove loss. Open seam or through-hole in a confirmed protective zone is actionable. |
| Waxed cotton | Wax treatment principally addresses resistance to water and wind. The Rokker garment tag in 011 and the Belstaff tag in 015 describe reproofing for weatherproofing. | **High** for care function. Worn wax or shell colour does not establish crash protection. Next evidence is the interior construction and the physical seam/layer in question. |
| Foam and component service life | Knox recommends regular inspection, replacement after damage/impact, and replacement of its protectors every 5 years. One published upgrade manual anchors service at date of purchase. Other protectors need their own instructions. | **High** for matched Knox products, **unknown** for an unidentified insert. A joint-protector CE tag or KNOX logo without a matching insert, applicable instructions and date yields a request for that evidence, not a whole-garment retirement rule. No verified generic shelf life for all leather jackets or all armour. |
| Pilling, thinning, holes | A visible hole establishes a local material discontinuity. A quilted thermal liner, mesh, and an abrasion layer are different physical objects. A fuzzy surface may be ordinary fabric nap. We found no garment-valid mapping from a photo pilling score or thinning estimate to an abrasion-time decrement. | **High** for the distinction; **unmeasured** for the decrement. Tag the exact physical layer and anatomical site. Native-resolution uncalibrated lining heads may raise review evidence. A confirmed, bound protective-area failure activates the hard shop gate. |

## Additional model inputs

Helite recommends checking its motorcycle airbag system and cartridge annually, whereas its published warranty for the airbag system is two years, with model/registration-dependent extensions. An overdue inspection is a service action, not a universal loss-of-protection date. No US garment-age rule establishing a two-year protection cutoff has been verified. **High** on the manufacturer's distinction. [Helite motorcycle FAQ](https://www.helite.com/airbag-systems-faqs/airbag-motorcycle/) · [US road-use context](https://www.nhtsa.gov/book/countermeasures-that-work/motorcycle-safety/countermeasures/other-strategies-behavior-change-0).

Polyurethane coatings can hydrolyse, delaminate or become sticky in some constructions. A maker documents this on its own waterproof outdoor products. Surface dots on an unknown nylon mesh are not an identification of PU or proof of hydrolysis. Polyamide type, leather/stitch condition and elastane fit are relevant observations; no validated wear-hour or calendar-life coefficients for the released garments were located. A different-coloured swatch cannot establish a quantitative UV dose or fibre strength: the original dye, exposed fibre, storage conditions and reference surface are unknown. Use a matched, unstressed area only for an explicitly uncalibrated observation. **Moderate** for cross-product coating mechanisms and **unknown** for specimen-level residual crash performance. [Sea to Summit on coatings](https://support.seatosummit.com/hc/en-us/articles/40429845208468-Can-I-use-an-aftermarket-product-to-make-a-dry-bag-waterproof-again) · [DuPont UV guidance](https://www.dupont.com/content/dam/dupont/amer/us/en/safety/public/documents/en/Kevlar_Technical_Guide_0319.pdf).

## Cases where the distinction changes the rider's next action

For item 002 the explicit production month is January 2020. It dates the garment. A replacement cycle published for another maker's armour does not apply to its jeans. For 015, the native image shows an opening with white batting in a quilted component. Ask for a connected view of where that component sits before calling it protective-area damage. For 017, there is a fractured corner on moulded shoulder hardware. The normal opening of the vent and its cracked corner are different observations. Seek a qualified check of the assembly's role. For 019, backlighting is visible through a regular perforated liner; the appearance alone does not prove a coating is flaking. Confidence high in the cited observations, conditional in their protective interpretation. Independent photo reference.
