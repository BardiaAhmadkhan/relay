# Standards on a garment label

Checked 21 November 2026. Input is text **bound to a garment or component**, including prefix, part, edition, pictogram, class and a source crop. Keep the source text even if two components disagree. The standard year describes an edition. A line explicitly identifying the production month describes manufacture. A brand establishment year supplies neither.

## What a jurisdiction changes

EU Regulation 2016/425 generally applied from 21 April 2018, with transition provisions including prior-directive products placed on the market before 21 April 2019. PPE was regulated before 2018 under Directive 89/686/EEC. The former professional-motorcyclist clothing standard predates the 17092 series. A tag image alone does not establish year of first EU placement, jurisdiction, exact declaration or noncompliance. Standards are a means of presumption of conformity; missing an EN number in a photo is missing evidence. Great Britain accepts CE or UKCA for PPE under current government guidance. Northern Ireland has different marking routes. Confidence high on cited dates/GB routes; individual legal eligibility requires more facts. [EU PPE Regulation, arts. 47–48](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/425/oj/eng) · [Earlier EU PPE Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/1989/686/oj/eng) · [UK market-marking table](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/product-regulations-by-sector-and-current-approaches-to-product-marking-ukca-and-ce-regimes/product-regulations-by-sector-and-current-approaches-to-product-marking-ukca-and-ce-regimes).

Canadian and US road-use guidance distinguishes mandatory motorcycle helmets from recommended apparel. We did not establish a single compulsory federal retail garment-class requirement equivalent to EN 17092 for every jacket/pants sale in either country; local and occupational rules can differ. Do not label a pictured product “uncertified,” noncompliant or North-American solely because a mark is missing. Place of manufacture and market of sale are separate facts. Confidence high in the distinction; broad negative legal claim excluded. [Québec road safety authority](https://saaq.gouv.qc.ca/en/road-safety/modes-transportation/motorcycle/helmets-protective-gear) · [NHTSA on protective clothing](https://www.nhtsa.gov/book/countermeasures-that-work/motorcycle-safety/countermeasures/other-strategies-behavior-change-0).
