# ADR-001 · Shared mobile client, replaceable vision, deterministic authority
Date: 2026-10-26. Status: selected for experiment; native runtime validation pending.

## Decision
Expo SDK 57/React Native/TypeScript plus a small local Node service and shared strict schemas/policy. Three initial views: front, back, label. Detail capture responds to uncertainty. Local CLIP/SigLIP are category evidence only. A structured cloud adapter can observe visible attributes/concerns but cannot certify or offer.

## Alternatives and evidence
See mobile-stack-decision and vision options research. Expo's SDK 57 docs expose CameraView and local image manipulation on iOS/Android, and official creation guidance recommends57 for ExpoGo during SDK 58 transition. Flutter exists only as a command shim in this environment; no runtime/toolchain advantage was verified. Native Swift+Kotlin requires two implementations and an unavailable Xcode host.
Sources: https://docs.expo.dev/get-started/create-a-project/ ; https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v57.0.0/sdk/camera/ ; https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v57.0.0/sdk/imagemanipulator/ . Accessed2026-10-26.

## Safety authority
Source: supplied organizer restrictions. Append-only rider disclosure events retain positive exclusions within an assessment; changing yes to no cannot erase them. No AI response owns a business decision. Every output has offerRecommended=false and protectivePerformanceCertified=false. A cloud observation may allow specialist offer review only; the experimental app does not issue offers.

## Tradeoffs
Local vision avoids per-call provider charges but cannot inspect condition and requires model download/local host. Multi-view capture adds work; three views are an experimental hypothesis pending expert/user validation. No automatic escalation/paid retries until a measured quality benefit and spending authorization exist.

## Revisit
Physical camera failure; SDK compatibility change; rubric changes; representative expert-labeled evidence favors another model or capture protocol. No claim of measured superiority before experiments.
