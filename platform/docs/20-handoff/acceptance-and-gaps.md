# Current acceptance boundary

The original failure is repaired at the pipeline level: usable single photos receive real local condition inspection even when history or other views are missing. All three supplied damaged jackets produced visible findings and a crash-like interpretation in real browser flows. The app has a revised mobile design, retained-photo notebook, cancellation/recovery controls and an installed Android emulator build.

This does not establish production readiness or reliable accident-history determination. The model still mistakes ordinary wear for damage, can mislocate features, and missed the first user photo and a source-described damaged detail in the 320-pixel stress run. Such low-detail empty results now request a clearer photograph. No visual result certifies protective performance or recommends an offer.

The small-specialist track has real exported models and native offline execution, with a verified encoder storage reduction. Its routing model still fails two important development examples and has not been activated. Damage-type, segmentation/extent and logo specialists are proposed tasks awaiting suitable labels, not hidden implemented capabilities.

Remaining requirements for daily business deployment: representative expert-labelled gear; fresh garment-separated evaluation; actual Android/iPhone camera, screen-reader and thermal/resource testing; reliable HTTPS service access or validated on-device inspection; retention/export requirements and multi-user ownership; organizer approval for event reuse. Browser/emulator results do not close these gates.

Cloud comparison is deliberately unperformed because the user chose local-only. The prior dependency review records two upstream Expo tooling advisories without a verified compatible fix. No public deployment, push, competition submission or organizer contact has occurred.
