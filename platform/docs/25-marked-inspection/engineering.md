## Capture and provenance

1. Capture or select the front or back. Relay saves its ordinary overview at up to 1600 pixels and keeps a reference to the oriented source during this draft session. The app measures the decoded dimensions. A restored draft uses its saved overview and labels it accordingly. The full-resolution original is held locally for this purpose. Only the prepared overview and selected crop enter the authenticated scoring path after the rider consents.
2. Choose **Mark an area for inspection**. Tap the mark or start at the centre and use the four movement controls. The tap maps through the rectangle actually occupied by the contained image. Taps in its letterbox and non-finite positions do not select pixels. The square and source-pixel count update before saving.
3. Small, Medium and Large treat the mark as 2.5%, 6% or 14% of the source's short edge. The selected contextual square is three times that width, at least 64 native pixels and at most the short edge. Its integer origin is clamped to the image. These three controls are usability starting points. The experiments establish the annotated three-times-mark crop as a useful input, while optimal human control sizes still require a rider study.
4. Extract from the oriented source at that integer rectangle. On native devices Expo ImageManipulator crops before resizing. The browser preview uses a canvas source rectangle. Both produce a 512 × 512 image for inspection. A 64-pixel crop contains 64 original pixels per side even when rendered at 512. The area remains linked to the overview. A separately photographed close-up has its own slot. The user can keep both.
5. A replacement mark gets a new photograph ID. Replacing an overview removes its derived mark from the active record. Removing an overview can be undone together with its mark. Archived brief references keep their files. Original camera and picker cache resources are released when superseded or when the draft session ends. After reopening, the recorded provenance still identifies the pixels used for the earlier mark. ## Scoring and review

The client scores the original first or reuses its current signed record. It sends the crop as a detail together with the original signature, view, ID, dimensions, source kind and rectangle. The service verifies the parent record and battery, enforces a front or back parent, and rejects self-links, invalid rectangles, wrong views, non-512-square chips and altered signatures. It scores the existing `broken` question directly on the marked pixels. The raw scorer is intentional. A singleton question sent through the ordinary early-exit plan can yield an empty answer. A signed child binds the crop byte hash, the submitted region and the verified parent byte hash. These checks establish record integrity. The camera supplies the relationship between the photograph and the physical item. [HTTP scoring path](../../server/app-v2.ts) · [API client](../../app/src/api-v2.ts)

A marked observation stays outside the ordinary coverage, category, grade and terminal damage calculations. Relay runs its original policy over the independent evidence first. A reported crash, protective-area damage or repair, or a qualifying original visual exclusion keeps its outcome. Missing coverage still requests the relevant view. An otherwise offer-eligible case with a rider-marked concern goes to specialist review. The phone also refuses an offer carrying signed marked evidence. High and low numerical crop responses have the same authority. This makes it possible to give a specialist the improved recognition signal without turning observed spot alarms into automatic exclusions. ## Integration and resource behavior

The model service uses a single shared execution lane for the existing plan and the new raw question. Both count toward periodic unloading. An unload waits for the current photograph to finish, a cancelled queued request does no model work, and a failed request releases the lane for its successor. Unmarked overviews add zero crop questions. Each saved mark adds one closed question on a cache miss. This is the method qualified on the original 4B checkpoint. None of the industrial weight edits enters this lane. [Shared lane](../../packages/vision/v2/inspection-lane.ts) · [Runtime tests](../../tests/marked-geometry.test.ts)

The health response carries an opaque context derived from the current signing key, scorer identities, working size, thresholds and plan configuration. The app reuses a stored score only in its matching context. In-flight scores are scoped to their assessment, photo and connection. A service that lacks marked-evidence support cannot silently assess that crop as an independent photograph. ## Qualification still needed for a shop

The numeric task was localized holes versus annotated spots in reused clothing. Field validation needs same-item overviews and fresh close-ups from riders and staff, across materials, phone cameras and locations. Include factory vents and perforations, mesh, logos, surface contamination, repairs in protective areas, left/right pairs, and known crash histories. Assign splits by physical garment, photographer and site. Measure tap error, source pixels, time, abandonment, missed exclusions, unnecessary referrals and case completion. Keep the current shop exclusions in force throughout. Compare paired assisted versus ordinary review with specialists blinded to the engine variant.
