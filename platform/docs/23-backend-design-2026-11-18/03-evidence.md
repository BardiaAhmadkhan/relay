### Contract trace

`app/src/session.ts` stores accepted native photos as up to 1600 px at JPEG quality 0.82. `app/src/useRelay.ts` detects a service and scores photos in the background when permitted on the service path. `server/app-v2.ts` prepares the photo, a 1024 px path for calibrated focus, and a low rendition when enabled. The planner picks a view-dependent set of questions. `packages/vision/v2/scorer-local.ts` reads the first token’s allowed-word probabilities, rejecting mass under 0.5. `scorer-chain.ts` handles retries, circuit breaking, cache and score signing. `packages/core/v2/policy.ts` evaluates history, visual hits, coverage, readability, ambiguity and grade. It can return a commercial offer in v2. The older v1 restrictions do not describe this implemented return path.

The current signed record includes pixel digest, photo ID, declared view, battery, scorer and answers. The phone’s reuse check primarily tests battery version. The API decision uses submitted photo records, history and signed scores. The optional review console re-prepares images. That split is why the new design binds item, generation, rendition/quality, destination and engine profile on the canonical assessment. The console queues cases in expiring process memory and appends verdict lines. Native text sharing is implemented. The inspected mobile client has no call to `/v2/review/submit`. A true cloud handoff needs delivery acknowledgment.

## 2. How much independent damage evidence exists

The E19 report evaluated 385 photographs, 22 identified as calibration overlaps and 363 reported as held out. It used assistant-made labels. The scoring service saw the real photograph. The outcome driver supplied three hypothetical “no” disclosures and replaced the required-view set with that photograph’s one view. Thus the 18 offers in that diagnostic are not 18 completed or verified eligible rider intakes.

| E19 cut | Photographs | Blind damaged positives | Observation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Historical configuration, excluding marked calibration overlaps | 363 | 11 | 10 block, one review, zero clear in the damage band. Five photographer-group identifiers contain all 11. |
| Remove the 22 `ov-` cases already present in the E14 candidate comparison | 341 | 5 | Six of the eleven original positives were among those earlier photos. |
| Also remove other photos from those E14 photographer groups | **339** | **3** | `cm-008` suit, `cm-057` gloves and `ox-093` jacket. All reached block. This removes known E14 overlap, not every possible exposure in project history. |
| Same narrower cut after the published post-score audit | 339 | 4 | Adds `cm-069`, a helmet. Keep this count separate from the blind analysis. |

The 363 include 213 scenes, 56 worn views, 68 item views, 16 detail views, 9 multi-item views and one label. On blind labels, **3/52** clean single-item photos reached damage block and 13/52 review. After two post-output label corrections, the rates are **2/50** blocked and 12/50 reviewed. Full-set held-out scenes mostly drew retakes (211/213), while 51/56 worn photos drew retakes. The single-item gate let 46/59 eligible single-item examples through in the prior analysis, missing 5/9 boot pairs. The one-item gate and some supporting thresholds default to 0.5 rather than a field fit.

Two E14 photographers supply eight of the eleven blind damage positives in E19. The independent exact binomial upper bound on zero failures among eleven *independent* cases would be 23.84% at one-sided 95%. Correlation weakens such an interpretation. On three independent positives it would be 63.16%. These fractions are illustrative uncertainty bounds for the respective case definition, not estimates of the frequency of unsafe commercial offers. An exact source manifest, physical item IDs, precommitted splits and a genuine matching front/back/label flow are high-value next investments. ## 3. New controlled checks against the current engine

### A. How much can fresh detail change apparent damage?

Joined every synthetic E18 mosaic at 1600 px to the pre-existing 1600 px score of its source photograph using `sourceId`. Both runs used the paired break/slide maximum and the same 0.145/0.646 full-size bands. Compare each mosaic with its original source image. The underlying parent has more detail and different framing. It is an oracle-style information comparison, not an automatic localization or capture result.

| Entire damaged mosaic | Source photo clear | Source photo review | Source photo block |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Clear | 3 | 6 | 36 |
| Review | 0 | 0 | 37 |
| Block | 0 | 0 | 11 |

**42/45 clear damaged mosaics** had a source photo in review or block. There were 93 damaged mosaics but only 31 distinct parent defects, all industrial fabric from one capture session. The 60 clean mosaics reached clear. Three diluted versions of one defect are correlated observations. Source detail also failed to flag three mosaic instances. The comparison does not guarantee that a good close-up solves every case. The case for asking for native detail is strongest when Relay can specify where and why, while preserving the overview. [E18 results](../../benchmarks/backend-v2/analysis/18-needle.txt)

### B. Three real photographs, two declared roles

Used a private loopback copy of the unmodified entry point, the current 4B digest and the default required views. Selected three **known diagnostic examples** before this run: an abraded shoe, a shop-display pair of scuffed boots and a museum fashion jacket. Submitted identical pixels as a front or a detail. Within each pair the canonical JPEG SHA matched. These six requests test sensitivity to the declared role. They do not estimate prevalence or reflect six independent items.

| Example | Detail / front damage band | Default disposition | What is learned |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ox-089`, shoe labeled damaged | Review / review | Retake for missing front / missing back respectively | The raw repair score was 0.342 as detail and 0.208 as front, crossing the default repair review threshold. Role-dependent scoring changed the evidence while the damage band stayed constant. |
| `cm-527`, boots labeled clean | Block / block | No offer both times | Front road-contact score 0.733. The early-exit plan returned only road contact and category before the terminal outcome. “Clean” is a visual annotation, not an in-hand or history finding. |
| `ox-140`, fashion jacket | Clear / clear | Retake for missing front / missing back respectively | The front received gear 0.797. The earlier one-photo experiment produced an offer. With the default view policy this live incomplete intake requested the missing view. |

The six photo requests took 569–7,417 ms here. They have different question paths and warm state. Do not quote their range as cloud latency. The labelled holdout’s prior front median was 3,904 ms. The exact reanalysis finds back median 4,108.5 ms, detail 1,630 ms and label 1,662 ms (one example). Earlier E17’s 1,824 ms median was a previous v3 profile and a selected 30-photo set.

### C. A deterministic gate for blurry or duplicated captures

Used the shipped focus functions on all 68 conventional held-out E19 item views. All 68 unmodified baseline soft flags reproduced. Applied controlled directional box blur on the normalized 512 grid using horizontal/vertical windows with edge replication. The original gate flagged 2/68. It flagged **30/68 horizontal, 27/68 vertical** at 11 px and **48/68 horizontal, 41/68 vertical** at 21 px. These are gate activations under known perturbations. Human legibility and damage-score sensitivity on these generated views were not measured. They motivate a regional, native motion/quality study without immediately increasing retake rates.

Separately fixed an 8×8 horizontal difference hash and Hamming distance ≤4 before comparing 363 source photos. It matched **363/363** JPEG quality-65 re-encodings. A 5% trim on every edge matched just **12/363**. No cross-ID pair fell within the threshold in 65,703 comparisons. Distinct IDs are not independently adjudicated distinct physical items. This is a cheap signal for a near-identical upload, unsuitable for claiming a new angle, locating a mark, or proving the two views show the same item.

### D. The repeated check and the actual warmup

Used the real pure policy, shipped thresholds, required front/back/label and valid synthetic observations. The complete clean fixture returned offer, grade B. An unknown crash answer returned review, and a declared protective-area exclusion returned no offer with zero photos. A 0.40 road-contact mark on a complete record requested detail. Appending a clear detail at the allowed attempt yielded review. A block-level detail yielded no offer. Advancing the prompt count on the same evidence yielded review. The client increments this count when it presents a detail/label-retake result. A low single-item score combined with a high road score also yielded no offer. These are reproducible logical transitions. They establish no image-recognition performance.

The actual service logged `scorer_warm` at 0 ms. Replaying its exact `GARMENT_QUESTIONS.slice(0, 1)` with the default planner and a counted inner scorer returned **zero inner calls, zero tokens, zero answers**. The sole supplied key is `usable`, while this planner begins with `road_contact`. The requested intersection is empty. This explains why a successful startup log cannot establish GPU readiness. Cold-start billing and first-rider latency need a probe that genuinely executes inference.

### E. Human-selected region on natural photographs

Picked three regions from a local contact sheet **before** scoring them: the right-hand helmet in a scene, one boot in the scuffed display, and the seam near the snap on a jacket labeled torn. For each, the control and crop used the same original photograph, JPEG quality 92 and original role. Normalized crop bounds, raw values and per-request times are in the ledger. The bounding boxes were selected by a person with access to the photograph, not proposed by Relay.

| Case | Whole → crop, same role | Damage band and default disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Helmet in a scene, `cm-091` | Single-item score 0.079 → 0.504; “broken” 0.610 → 0.212. Road-contact 0.014 → 0.324 | Review → review. Requested missing back both times. The crop clarified subject identity but changed which damage cue dominated. |
| Visually intact scuffed boots, `cm-527` | Road-contact 0.770 → 0.649 | Block → block. No offer twice. The cropped value remained just above 0.646. Cropping failed to resolve the false visual exclusion. |
| Torn jacket seam, `ox-093` | Broken-material score 0.886 → 0.756. Repair 0.488 → 0.273 | Block → block. No offer twice. The underlying break stayed visible. The repair answer crossed a review threshold. |

**Zero of three damage bands changed.** A crop may make the subject clearer while leaving wear/defect confusion intact. A different damage cue can rise as another falls. This is evidence against promising that “zooming in” will automatically improve field accuracy. It supports presenting the physical relationship to a reviewer, measuring the value of a genuinely new photo, and keeping a second image as a candidate source of information. The E19 `cm-091` band on its original bytes and runtime state differed from this JPEG92 whole control. Only the matched control/crop is the tested contrast here.

### F. Is an earlier framing check worth buying?

Replayed the existing `singleItem < 0.5` signal across every conventional held-out front/back view. This was a scheduling thought experiment using existing observations, with no altered thresholds or new predictions. Of 346 overviews, 335 had a score and 282 triggered. Among the triggers the historical one-photo outcome was retake for 281 and no offer for one. These requests had a median 4,004.5 ms of service time. Reordering still costs time to run the framing gate, so the logged duration is **not** a savings estimate.

Of 213 scenes, 212 triggered. Among 68 isolated item photographs, 59 had a score and 13 triggered. Among seven blind-damaged overviews, only one had a recorded single-item score and none of those observed triggered. Early exit censors the rest. This is evidence for *benchmarking* an early framing lane, against evidence for separating legitimate pairs and retaining holds on any suspicious material. A corpus with 213 scenes inflates the apparent opportunity relative to a properly guided shop intake.

## 6. What remains unmeasured

Real protective-area repair sensitivity. True item-level unsafe recommendation rate. Independent dual-expert agreement. Full multiview native intakes with known physical histories. Two-phone native cloud parity. Job throughput/tail latency and paid cloud cost. Actual rider follow-through and specialist time saved. The proposed automatic resolution and same-size weight variants have no claim to improvement yet. Each has an experiment and a fallback in the [qualification plan](02-build-and-qualification.md).
